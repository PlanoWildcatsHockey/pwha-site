*Oct. 25, 2026 · US Rink, Plano*

[Isiah Fowler](/players/isiah-fowler/) scored a shorthanded goal with 1:29 left to give Plano a 4–3 win after Birdville came back from three goals down.

Plano built its lead on very few shots: the score sheet credits Plano with only three shots through two periods, and three of them went in. [Kai Hu](/players/kai-hu/) scored on the power play in the first period and again in the second, both assisted by Fowler. [Hudson Mun](/players/hudson-mun/) added a third later in the second, from Fowler and [Ain Rubio](/players/ain-rubio/), for a 3–0 lead.

Birdville scored three times in the third. George Ebert started it, and Zakary Patterson scored twice, the second with 3:33 left, to tie the game 3–3. With Plano killing a penalty, Fowler won it, assisted by Mun and [David Yu](/players/david-yu/). Fowler finished with a goal and three assists.

Birdville outshot Plano 26–22, and [Raudeen Roodgarmi](/players/raudeen-roodgarmi/) made 23 saves in goal.
