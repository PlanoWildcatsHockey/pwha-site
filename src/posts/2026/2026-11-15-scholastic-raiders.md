*Nov. 15, 2026 · World Rink, Plano*

The Raiders beat the scholastic team 6–1.

The Raiders led 2–0 after one period and 3–0 after two, then scored three more in the third. Kai Scullin scored twice for the Raiders. Matthew McGee scored Plano's goal with 1:24 left, assisted by Mykhailo Paziuk.

The Raiders outshot Plano 24–11, and Ayden Shobe made 18 saves in goal.
