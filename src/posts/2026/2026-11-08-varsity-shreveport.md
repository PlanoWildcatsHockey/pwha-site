*Nov. 8, 2026 · Blue Rink, Richardson*

[Hudson "No Mercy" Mun](/players/hudson-mun/) scored the winner as Plano beat Shreveport 7–6 in a game that was tied four times.

Shreveport scored three times in the first period, two of them from Reid Fontenot, to lead 3–1. Plano came back before the intermission. [Iverson Herr](/players/iverson-herr/) scored on the power play, and [Kai Hu](/players/kai-hu/) scored on a penalty shot to tie it 3–3. Hu had also scored Plano's first goal, assisted by [Ain Rubio](/players/ain-rubio/).

[Isiah Fowler](/players/isiah-fowler/) gave Plano its first lead early in the second on another power play, from [Braeden Ysasaga](/players/braeden-ysasaga/) and Hu. Shreveport's Jacob Raines tied it, and [Carter Doyle](/players/carter-doyle/) put Plano back in front, 5–4. Jake Tew tied it again early in the third, and [Ethan Barton](/players/ethan-barton/) put Plano back ahead 6–5 with 5:27 left. Mun added a seventh goal for a 7–5 lead, and Shreveport scored once more to make it 7–6, but Plano held on.

A late-game altercation led to majors and misconducts for both teams. [Raudeen Roodgarmi](/players/raudeen-roodgarmi/) started in goal for Plano, and [Andrew Drake](/players/andrew-drake/) played the second and third periods.

The league score sheet is missing the last two goals.
