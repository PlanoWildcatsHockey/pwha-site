*Nov. 8, 2026 · Blue Rink, Richardson*

Ethan Barton scored with 5:27 left to give Plano a 6–5 win over Shreveport in a game that was tied four times.

Shreveport scored three times in the first period, two of them from Reid Fontenot, to lead 3–1. Plano came back before the intermission. Iverson Herr scored on the power play, and Kai Hu scored on a penalty shot to tie it 3–3. Hu had also scored Plano's first goal, assisted by Ain Rubio.

Isiah Fowler gave Plano its first lead early in the second on another power play, from Braeden Ysasaga and Hu. Shreveport's Jacob Raines tied it, and Carter Doyle put Plano back in front, 5–4. Jake Tew tied it again early in the third before Barton scored the winner.

A late-game altercation led to majors and misconducts for both teams in the final five minutes. Shreveport outshot Plano 27–26, and Plano's goaltender made 22 saves.
