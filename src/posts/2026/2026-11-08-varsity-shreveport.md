*Nov. 8, 2026 · Blue Rink, Richardson*

Hudson "No Mercy" Mun scored the winner as Plano beat Shreveport 7–6 in a game that was tied four times.

Shreveport scored three times in the first period, two of them from Reid Fontenot, to lead 3–1. Plano came back before the intermission. Iverson Herr scored on the power play, and Kai Hu scored on a penalty shot to tie it 3–3. Hu had also scored Plano's first goal, assisted by Ain Rubio.

Isiah Fowler gave Plano its first lead early in the second on another power play, from Braeden Ysasaga and Hu. Shreveport's Jacob Raines tied it, and Carter Doyle put Plano back in front, 5–4. Jake Tew tied it again early in the third, and Ethan Barton put Plano back ahead 6–5 with 5:27 left. Mun added a seventh goal for a 7–5 lead, and Shreveport scored once more to make it 7–6, but Plano held on.

A late-game altercation led to majors and misconducts for both teams. Raudeen Roodgarmi started in goal for Plano, and Andrew Drake played the second and third periods.

The league score sheet is missing the last two goals.
