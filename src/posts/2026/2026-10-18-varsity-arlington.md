*Oct. 18, 2026 · Morrow Rink, Mansfield*

Plano outshot Arlington 36–33 and pulled within a goal in the third period, but Arlington held on for a 5–3 win.

The first period ended in a flurry. Arlington's Aidan Anderson scored with 2:11 left, Iverson Herr tied it 41 seconds later, and Joseph Bishop put Arlington back in front with 20 seconds left. Arlington scored twice in 49 seconds midway through the second, through Nathan Martinez and Milo Lazatin, to lead 4–1. Hudson Mun answered with 2:22 left in the period.

William Wallace made it 4–3 midway through the third, but Anderson scored his second of the game with 3:32 left to seal it.

Plano's goaltender made 28 saves.
