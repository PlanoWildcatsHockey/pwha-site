Practices begin Monday, August 17 at 8:15 PM. A parent meeting will be held before practice at 7:45 PM to discuss the upcoming season, answer questions, and talk about fundraising opportunities.

REGISTRATION DEADLINE: Both steps below must be completed by Monday, August 17 — before practice.

REGISTRATION IS TWO-FOLD

1. [Register with the TSHL in Hockey Shift](https://my.hockeyshift.com/#/register/22996)
You will likely need to create an account and then add a profile for your player. You pay nothing to the league.


2. [Register with the Plano Wildcats](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScsGsA7Zy6ulK5aASZimaLSX_UQkMeBj3K7xvm0wi2eeWaKFg/viewform?usp=preview)

WHAT ABOUT COST?

Unfortunately, costs are higher this year than last, as previously mentioned.

Team Fees:

Player — Scholastic (JV): $2,300 | Varsity: $2,700
Goalie — Scholastic (JV): $1,600 | Varsity: $1,900

One-Time Costs (if needed):

- New Uniform Set: $200 (2 jerseys, breezers, socks)
- New Equipment Set: $450 (white helmet, maroon gloves)
- New Practice Jerseys: $50 (set of two, or $25 each)

Single Full Payment includes the Team Fee plus any one-time costs that apply. For example, a new Scholastic player may owe $2,300 + $200 + $450 + $50 = $3,000, and a new Varsity player may owe $2,700 + $200 + $450 + $50 = $3,400.

Available Team Fee Payment Plan:

- Due 9/11/26 (day before first Leveling game) — Players: $1,300 Scholastic / $1,700 (Varsity); Goalies: $600 Scholastic / $900 (Varsity)
- Due 10/15/2026 — $500
- Due 11/15/2026 — $500
