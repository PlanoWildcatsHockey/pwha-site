*Nov. 18, 2026 · Gaglardi Rink, Farmers Branch*

Plano Senior varsity outshot Highland Park 48–38 and erased two one-goal deficits, but lost 4–3 in the fourth round of the shootout.

[Hudson Mun](/players/hudson-mun/) opened the scoring midway through the first period, finishing a feed from [Kai Hu](/players/kai-hu/) for a 1–0 Plano lead after one. Highland Park scored twice in the second period, through Saarav Pandey and Benjamin Fleiss, to go ahead. [Carter Doyle](/players/carter-doyle/) tied it 2–2 with 2:39 left in the period. Highland Park's Sloan Shea scored with 34 seconds left, so Plano went into the second intermission down 3–2.

Plano controlled the third period and outshot Highland Park 19–6. Mun scored his second goal of the night with 2:59 left in regulation to tie it 3–3. [Andrew Drake](/players/andrew-drake/) made 35 saves, including key stops in an overtime where Highland Park outshot Plano 6–2, to get Plano to a shootout.

Pandey scored for Highland Park in the first round. Mun answered in the third round to level the shootout, but Tristan Eastep scored in the fourth round to win it for Highland Park.

Plano took only one penalty all night, an interference minor on [Ethan Barton](/players/ethan-barton/) in the third period.
