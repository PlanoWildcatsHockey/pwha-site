*Oct. 4, 2026 · World Rink, Plano*

The scholastic team trailed Keller 6–0 after two periods, then scored four goals in the third before losing 6–4.

Keller scored three times in each of the first two periods, and Jackson Clay finished with a hat trick. Plano outshot Keller 10–5 in the third period and came back. Abel Peperis scored early in the period, and Nolan O'Neill scored 52 seconds later, assisted by Gram Gatewood. Martin Zavala-Dzhyoyeva scored with 1:23 left, from Gianni Kelly, and William Wallace scored with 23 seconds left.

Keller outshot Plano 22–18 overall, and Plano's goaltender made 16 saves.
