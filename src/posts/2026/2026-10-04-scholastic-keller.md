*Oct. 4, 2026 · World Rink, Plano*

The Wildcats trailed Keller 6–0 after two periods, then scored four goals in the third before losing 6–4.

Keller scored three times in each of the first two periods, and Jackson Clay finished with a hat trick. Plano outshot Keller 10–5 in the third period and came back. [Abel Peperis](/players/abel-peperis/) scored early in the period, and [Nolan O'Neill](/players/nolan-o-neill/) scored 52 seconds later, assisted by [Gram Gatewood](/players/gram-gatewood/). [Martin Zavala-Dzhyoyeva](/players/martin-zavala-dzhyoyeva/) scored with 1:23 left, from [Gianni Kelly](/players/gianni-kelly/), and [William Wallace](/players/william-wallace/) scored with 23 seconds left.

Keller outshot Plano 22–18 overall, [Isaac Bywater](/players/isaac-bywater/) started in goal and [Ayden Shobe](/players/ayden-shobe/) finished the game; they combined for 16 saves.
