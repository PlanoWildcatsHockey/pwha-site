*Sept. 13, 2026 · Gaglardi Rink, Farmers Branch*

The scholastic team closed its leveling weekend with a 5–1 win over Highland Park, hours after losing to Prosper. [Carter Doyle](/players/carter-doyle/) and [William Wallace](/players/william-wallace/) each scored twice.

[Abel Peperis](/players/abel-peperis/) opened the scoring in the first period. Doyle made it 2–0 in the second, assisted by [Gram Gatewood](/players/gram-gatewood/), before Highland Park's Logan Veski scored late in the period. Doyle scored again in the third. Wallace added an empty-net goal with 1:53 left and another, from [Dominic Martinez](/players/dominic-martinez/), with 21 seconds to go.

Shots were scarce through two periods: Plano had two and Highland Park one. Plano took over in the third, outshooting Highland Park 30–21, and [Ayden Shobe](/players/ayden-shobe/) made 21 saves in goal.
