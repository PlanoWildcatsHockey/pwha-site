*Nov. 22, 2026 · Morrow Rink, Mansfield*

The scholastic team came back from 5–2 down in the third period but fell to Mansfield 5–4.

Abel Peperis tied the game 1–1 in the first period, and Liam McCurry put Mansfield ahead 2–1 before the intermission. After a scoreless second, Mansfield scored twice early in the third to lead 4–1. Peperis scored his second goal, Allen Lile answered for Mansfield, and William Wallace scored 14 seconds after that to make it 5–3. Wallace scored again with 3:36 left, assisted by Nolan O'Neill and Flynn Plate, but Plano couldn't find the tying goal.

Mansfield outshot Plano 44–23, and Plano's goaltender made 39 saves.
