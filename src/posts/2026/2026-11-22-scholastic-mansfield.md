*Nov. 22, 2026 · Morrow Rink, Mansfield*

The scholastic team came back from 5–2 down in the third period but fell to Mansfield 5–4.

[Abel Peperis](/players/abel-peperis/) tied the game 1–1 in the first period, and Liam McCurry put Mansfield ahead 2–1 before the intermission. After a scoreless second, Mansfield scored twice early in the third to lead 4–1. Peperis scored his second goal, Allen Lile answered for Mansfield, and [William Wallace](/players/william-wallace/) scored 14 seconds after that to make it 5–3. Wallace scored again with 3:36 left, assisted by [Nolan O'Neill](/players/nolan-o-neill/) and [Flynn Plate](/players/flynn-plate/), but Plano couldn't find the tying goal.

Mansfield outshot Plano 44–23, and [Isaac Bywater](/players/isaac-bywater/) made 39 saves in goal.
