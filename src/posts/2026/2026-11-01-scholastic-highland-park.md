*Nov. 1, 2026 · Gaglardi Rink, Farmers Branch*

[Ayden Shobe](/players/ayden-shobe/) stopped all 39 shots through regulation and overtime, and [Flynn Plate](/players/flynn-plate/) scored in the seventh round of the shootout to give the Wildcats a 1–0 win over Highland Park.

Highland Park outshot Plano 39–14, but neither team scored in regulation or overtime. In the shootout, [Mykhailo Paziuk](/players/mykhailo-paziuk/) scored for Plano in the second round and Philipp Kormilitsin answered for Highland Park. The next four rounds were scoreless before Plate scored and Shobe made the final stop.
