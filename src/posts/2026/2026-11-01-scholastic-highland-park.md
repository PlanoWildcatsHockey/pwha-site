*Nov. 1, 2026 · Gaglardi Rink, Farmers Branch*

Plano's goaltender stopped all 39 shots through regulation and overtime, and Flynn Plate scored in the seventh round of the shootout to give the scholastic team a 1–0 win over Highland Park.

Highland Park outshot Plano 39–14, but neither team scored in regulation or overtime. In the shootout, Mykhailo Paziuk scored for Plano in the second round and Philipp Kormilitsin answered for Highland Park. The next four rounds were scoreless before Plate scored and Plano's goaltender made the final stop.
