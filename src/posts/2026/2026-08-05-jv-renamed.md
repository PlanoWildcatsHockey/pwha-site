An update from the TSHL:

The Texas Scholastic Hockey League is announcing a change to the classification and naming of our current Junior Varsity division.

Following further review of USA Hockey and Texas Amateur Hockey Association (TAHA) eligibility requirements, we determined that the traditional Junior Varsity (JV) designation is not the best fit for our league. USA Hockey and TAHA high school hockey regulations do not permit 7th grade students to participate in high school hockey, while our developmental division is intended to provide opportunities for eligible younger scholastic players in addition to high school students.

To better reflect the purpose of this division and eliminate any confusion regarding eligibility, the Junior Varsity Division will be renamed the TSHL **Scholastic Division**.

The **Scholastic Division** will operate under its own league rules and eligibility requirements designed specifically for this level of competition while remaining an important part of the Texas Scholastic Hockey League.

For our member clubs, this change should have minimal operational impact. The primary change will be how teams are referenced and marketed. Clubs should begin referring to these teams as **TSHL Scholastic teams** rather than Junior Varsity teams in communications, promotional materials, and on their websites.

This reclassification better aligns our league structure with USA Hockey and TAHA regulations while providing a clear identity for a division focused on developing scholastic hockey players.

Additional information regarding **Scholastic Division** rules and eligibility will be distributed as part of the league rules.
