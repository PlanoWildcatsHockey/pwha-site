*Nov. 8, 2026 · Lehtinen Rink, Euless*

Birdville scored five times in the third period to beat the Wildcats 7–1.

Birdville's Cooper Leggett scored twice in the first period. [Abel Peperis](/players/abel-peperis/) cut it to 2–1 in the second, and it stayed that way into the third. Birdville then pulled away, and Leggett finished with four goals.

Birdville outshot Plano 64–12, and [Isaac Bywater](/players/isaac-bywater/) made 57 saves in goal.
