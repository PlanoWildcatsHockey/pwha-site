*Nov. 8, 2026 · Lehtinen Rink, Euless*

Birdville scored five times in the third period to beat the scholastic team 7–1.

Birdville's Cooper Leggett scored twice in the first period. Abel Peperis cut it to 2–1 in the second, and it stayed that way into the third. Birdville then pulled away, and Leggett finished with four goals.

Birdville outshot Plano 64–12, and Isaac Bywater made 57 saves in goal.
