We crashed early Saturday morning practice and asked one question: what's your favorite breakfast?

The team came in strong with scrambled eggs (extra cheese), omelettes, bacon and pancakes, chocolatey cereal, waffles, and not one but *three* Chick-fil-A orders. One guy admitted he doesn't really eat breakfast at all.

Then we asked Coach Derrick… and he went straight for the drive-thru.
