A reminder from the TSHL Commissioner:

**2008 and 2009 birth year players** that they must complete their mandatory [SafeSport Training](https://www.usahockey.com/safesporttraining) as soon as possible.

**Important Information:**

- **Eligibility Warning:** Players who have not completed their training by leveling will be unable to participate in those games.
- **Portal Delay Notice:** Please note that completions are currently taking extra time to reflect in the USA Hockey portal system. We strongly encourage everyone to finish this right away to avoid any clearance delays prior to games.
