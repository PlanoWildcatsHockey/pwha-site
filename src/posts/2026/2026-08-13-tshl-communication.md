TSHL Commissioner Zee Howard asked clubs to share this with our families:

**TSHL Communication Protocol**



**Our Shared Purpose**

To ensure players, families, and programs receive accurate, consistent, and timely guidance, the Texas Scholastic Hockey League (TSHL) utilizes a structured Chain of Communication.

By filtering everyday inquiries through designated Club Representatives, we empower local club leadership to support their own families, protect our volunteers' time, and guarantee that official league responses remain consistent across all programs.

To maintain this balance, the TSHL Commissioner will communicate exclusively with designated Club Representatives. Direct operational inquiries sent to the Commissioner from individual parents, players, or team staff will be kindly redirected back to local club leadership.

**The Chain of Communication**

For routine operational questions, scheduling, rule interpretations, and general league matters, please follow this 4-step path:

**Step 1: Reach Out to Your Club Representative:**  **Players & Families**

If you have questions about team operations, rules, or schedules, please start by contacting your coach, team manager, or designated Club Representative. Your club is best equipped to handle day-to-day details and assist you directly.

**Step 2: League Escalation:**  **Club Leadership**

If an issue cannot be resolved at the club level, or requires official league interpretation, your designated Club Representative will reach out directly to the TSHL Commissioner.

**Step 3: Commissioner Review:**  **League Commissioner**

The Commissioner will review the matter with the Club Representative and provide guidance. If the topic involves broad policy precedents, formal appeals, or major league decisions, the Commissioner will bring the matter to the Board of Governors.

**Step 4: Final Determination:**  **Board of Governors**

For matters elevated to the Board, a final determination will be made and shared through the Commissioner back to the Club Representative, who will then inform the affected parties.

**Exception: Matters Involving Club Leadership & Ethics**

We recognize that certain sensitive situations require an independent reporting path outside of local club leadership.  If an inquiry, grievance, or safety concern specifically involves your program's Club Leadership, or represents an ethics concern that cannot be handled internally at the club level, families are welcome to reach out directly to the Board of Governors,**[governor@texasscholastichockeyleague.com](mailto:governor@texasscholastichockeyleague.com)**

Upon receipt, the Board of Governors will review the submission and, if appropriate, refer the matter directly to the **TSHL Ethics Committee** for independent evaluation and resolution.

**Friendly Reminder Regarding Direct Inquiries**

To ensure every family receives accurate and official guidance, and to keep club leadership fully informed, direct emails, calls, or text messages sent to the Commissioner from individual parents or players will receive an automated reply redirecting them to their Club Representative (or to the Board of Governors for leadership/ethics concerns).

Thank you for your partnership and cooperation as we work together to support a fair, smooth, and enjoyable season for all of our players!

Zee
