Spirit wear ordering is open for Fall 2026. Shirts, hoodies, hats, yard signs, and car decals — all with your choice of logo. Get your order in before Labor Day.

## What's Available

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| T-Shirt — Black or Maroon | $15 |
| Hoodie — Black or Maroon | $25 |
| Richardson Trucker Hat | $30 |
| 24" x 24" Yard Sign | $25 |
| 5" Circle Car Decal | $10 |

T-shirts and hoodies come in Black or Maroon and sizes Small Adult through 2XL — use a separate line on the order form for each color you want. Any shirt or hoodie can be paired with any of the 4 logos shown on the flyer above; just write the logo number (1–4) on the form. If you're ordering a yard sign or car decal, include the player's name and number for personalization.

## How to Order

1. Download and fill out the order form below.
2. Choose your payment method — check (payable to Plano Wildcats Hockey Association), Zelle (treasurer@planowildcatshockey.com or "wildcatshockey"), PayPal (@planowildcatshockey — please add 3% to cover the fee), or cash.
3. Email the completed form to [varsitymanager@planowildcatshockey.com](mailto:varsitymanager@planowildcatshockey.com).
