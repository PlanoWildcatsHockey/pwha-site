*Sept. 27, 2026 · NYTEX Sports Centre (West Rink)*

[Kai Hu](/players/kai-hu/) scored all three Plano goals in the varsity season opener, but Southlake Carroll pulled away for a 5–3 win.

Southlake Carroll's Austin Bierwirth opened the scoring in the first period, and Hu answered 27 seconds later from [Hudson Mun](/players/hudson-mun/) to make it 1–1 after one. Southlake Carroll took over in the second period, scoring twice in the same minute early in the period and adding a second goal from Davis Heikkinen to lead 4–1 at the second intermission.

Hu cut it to 4–2 midway through the third, but Mason Dispensa restored the three-goal lead 32 seconds later. Hu completed his hat trick with 1:54 left, assisted by [Iverson Herr](/players/iverson-herr/).

Southlake Carroll outshot Plano 40–16, and [Raudeen Roodgarmi](/players/raudeen-roodgarmi/) made 35 saves in goal.
