[AT&T Metroplex HSHL](https://www.atthighschoolhockeyleague.com) is currently finalizing the game dates for the fall/winter season. Here are the tentative highlights:

**2026-2027 Game Dates**

- September 12-13: Leveling Showcase
- September 23: Game 1 Varsity Gold & Silver
- September 27: Game 1 Varsity Bronze and all JV
- Playoffs: Varsity Gold & Silver (weeknight games)
- March 6-7: Playoffs — Varsity Bronze & all JV
