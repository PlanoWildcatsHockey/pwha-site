*Nov. 1, 2026 · US Rink, Plano*

Plano trailed Mansfield 5–3 after two periods and won 7–6. Kai Hu scored four goals, Braeden Ysasaga had six assists, and Carter Doyle scored the winner with 2:05 left.

The teams traded goals for two periods. Hu tied it 1–1 in the first, then scored again in the second, and Isiah Fowler added another. Each time, Mansfield answered, and Jacob Pollifrone's goal with 2:15 left in the second made it 5–3.

Plano took over in the third. Hu scored midway through the period, and Iverson Herr tied it 5–5 just 17 seconds later. Hu put Plano ahead with 3:41 left, but Mansfield's Connor Heverly tied it again less than a minute later. Doyle answered 39 seconds after that, assisted by Brett Riska and Ysasaga, and Plano held on.

Mansfield outshot Plano 30–25, and Plano's goaltender made 24 saves.
