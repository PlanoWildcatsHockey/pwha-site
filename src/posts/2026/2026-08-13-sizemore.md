Coach Jason Sizemore has let us know that he needs to step back from coaching this season to focus on work and family. The Board joins our coaches in thanking him for everything he poured into the Wildcats JV team over the years, including leading them to the 2024 Championship. We'll miss his love for the game and the players, and we wish him all the best.

Coach Derrick is working on filling the coaching positions, and we'll share updates as soon as we have them.
