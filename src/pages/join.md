## Eligibility

### Age & Grade

To play high school hockey in the 2026–2027 season, a player must meet **both** of the following:

- **Birth year:** 2013 or earlier (players born in 2014 or later are not eligible)
- **Grade:** Entering 7th grade or higher in fall 2026

Additional limits by team:

| Team | Eligible Grades | Birth Year Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Scholastic | Entering 7th–10th grade | 2010 or later (16U max) |
| Varsity | Entering 9th–12th grade | 2009 or earlier only |

No 7th or 8th graders may play Varsity. No 11th or 12th graders may play Scholastic. A player who turns 19 before September 1 is not eligible.

### Where You Play

Every eligible player in the DFW Metroplex may play high school hockey. Team assignment is based on where you live or what school you attend:

- **Attendance zone:** If you live within a zone affiliated with a club, you play for that club.
- **Private school:** If you attend a private school with its own team, you may play for that school's team.
- **Home school / unaffiliated school:** Play for the club affiliated with your ISD.

## Before You Register

Complete these two steps **before** showing up to tryouts.

### 1. USA Hockey Membership

All players must have a valid 2026–2027 USA Hockey membership number before registering with PWHA.  _The 4th digit must be the number 7._

[Register with USA Hockey](https://www.usahockey.com/membership){.button data-button-variant="primary"}

### 2. SafeSport Training

Players turning **17 or older in 2026** (2009 and prior birth years) are required to complete SafeSport training online. This is a USA Hockey mandate — players cannot participate in games, practices, or be rostered until it's done. Complete it every year.

Have your USA Hockey registration number ready before starting.

[Complete SafeSport Training](https://www.usahockey.com/safesporttraining){.button}
