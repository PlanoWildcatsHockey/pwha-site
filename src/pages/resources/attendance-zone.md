The Plano Wildcats serve players in the **[Plano Senior High School](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/7478) attendance zone**. The feeder schools for this zone are:

- Middle: [Schimelpfenig](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/8662)
- Elementary: [Bethany](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/1706), [Carlisle](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/2446), [Christie](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/2742), [Mathews](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/5850)
- Middle: [Hendrick](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/4518)
- Elementary: [Beverly](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/1854), [Hedgcoxe](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/4370), [Rasor](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/7774), [Thomas](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/9550)

**[Vines High School](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/9698) feeder schools:**
- Middle: [Wilson](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/10290)
- Elementary: [Aldridge](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/966), [Jackson](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/5554) (east of Coit), [Shepard](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/8810), [Sigler](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/9106), [Weatherford](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/9846)
- Middle: [Haggard](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/3778)
- Elementary: [Harrington](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/3926), [Hughston](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/5110), [Saigling](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/8366), [Wells](https://www.pisd.edu/fs/pages/9994)

Not sure if you're in the zone? [Contact us](/contact/) and we'll help you figure it out.
