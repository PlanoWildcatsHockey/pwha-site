All Plano Wildcats players, coaches, team officials, parents, spectators, and fans are required to adhere to the conduct standards established by USA Hockey, the Texas Amateur Hockey Association (TAHA), StarCenters, and PWHA. These standards apply during games, practices, tournaments, and any activities at StarCenters facilities or under USA Hockey sanctioning.

## Standards

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Verbal abuse or harassment directed at officials, players, coaches, staff, or spectators
- Profanity, threatening language, or discriminatory remarks
- Unsportsmanlike conduct on or off the ice
- Failure to comply with facility policies or staff direction
- Escalation of conflicts in stands, benches, locker rooms, or common areas

Clubs are responsible for the conduct of their players, coaches, and spectators. Immediate corrective action is expected when violations occur.

## Consequences

Failure to comply may result in:

- Removal of individuals from the facility
- Game suspensions or forfeitures
- Loss of ice privileges
- Formal review and additional sanctions by governing bodies

## Our commitment

Our shared responsibility is to provide a safe, respectful, and development-focused environment for all participants.
