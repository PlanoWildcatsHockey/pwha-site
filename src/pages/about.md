# About PWHA

The Plano Wildcats Hockey Association (PWHA) is the parent booster organization that supports the Plano Senior High School hockey program — Varsity and Junior Varsity teams, players, families, and alumni.

PWHA is a **student-initiated, non-curricular club**. It is not affiliated with Plano ISD or Plano Senior High School, and is not monitored, approved, or promoted by any Plano ISD employee.

We compete in the [AT&T Metroplex High School Hockey League](https://www.atthighschoolhockeyleague.com/), one of the largest high school hockey leagues in the country. Our home rink is **Children's Health StarCenter Plano**, 4020 W Plano Pkwy.
