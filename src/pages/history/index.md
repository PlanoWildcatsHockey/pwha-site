The Plano Wildcats are one of the founding programs of high school hockey in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. The 2026-2027 season marks the **30th full season** of organized high school hockey in DFW. Play began in early 1997 with just four teams, including Plano.

In that first season, Plano defeated [Jesuit](https://jesuitrangers.org/sports/mens-ice-hockey) in the final. From those four original teams the league has grown into one of the largest high school hockey leagues in the country, with dozens of programs competing across multiple divisions. Plano has been part of it since the beginning.

The Wildcats compete in the **Bronze** division of the Texas Scholastic Hockey League, fielding both Varsity and Scholastic (JV) teams. Players come from Plano Senior High School and it's feeder schools: Clark High School, Vines High School, Haggard Middle School, Hendrick Middle School, Wilson Middle School, and Schimelpfenig Middle School.
