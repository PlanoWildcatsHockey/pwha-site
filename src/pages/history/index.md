# Program History

The Plano Wildcats are one of the founding programs of high school hockey in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. The 2026-2027 season marks the **30th full season** of the AT&T Metroplex High School Hockey League — a league that began in early 1997 with just four teams, including Plano.

In that first season, Plano defeated Jesuit in the final. From those four original teams the league has grown into one of the largest high school hockey leagues in the country, with dozens of programs competing across multiple divisions. Plano has been part of it since the beginning.

The Wildcats compete in the **Lower Varsity East** division of the AT&T Metroplex HSHL, fielding both Varsity and Junior Varsity teams. Players come from Plano Senior High School and surrounding Clark High School.
