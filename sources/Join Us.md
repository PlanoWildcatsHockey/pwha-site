## LEAGUE ELIGIBILITY RULES

To be eligible to play High School Hockey for the 2026 Summer and 26-27 Fall/Winter season you must be a 2013 birth year or older. 2014 birth years & younger are not eligible for high school hockey. Additionally, you must also be entering the 7th grade or higher in the fall of 2026. If you meet BOTH requirements, please read further.

Every player in the DFW Metroplex that meets the above requirements is eligible to play High School Hockey. Where you play is based primarily on where you live. If you reside in an area that is affiliated with a club then you play for that club, unless you attend a private school that has a team. If you are home schooled or attend a school that is not affiliated with a club but reside in an ISD that is affiliated with a Club, then you play for that club.

Players entering 7th

-10th grade will be eligible to play JV. Players entering 9th

-12th grade will be eligible to play Varsity. No 7th or 8th graders may play Varsity and no 11th or 12th graders may play JV. A player who turns 19 prior to September 1st is not eligible to play High School Hockey. The maximum birth year to play JV is 2010(16U). A player 2009 or older is not eligible for JV.

## SafeSport Training

All players turning 17 or Older in 2026 will be required to complete the on-line SafeSport Training.  This is a USA Hockey requirement, and players will not be able to play or practice or be rostered until this training is complete. Players should have their USAH registration number prior to taking the SafeSport Training. Below is a link to the SafeSport Training. You must take this every year.

- [SAFESPORT TRAINING](https://www.usahockey.com/safesporttraining)
